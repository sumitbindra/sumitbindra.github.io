## Background

Before diving into AI and startups, I spent 8 years as a transportation consultant at **RSG**, working on complex systems modeling -- large-scale simulations of how millions of people make travel decisions. That work in traffic engineering, discrete choice modeling, and behavioral forecasting built the foundation for my current work in AI. Modeling how humans make decisions turns out to be surprisingly relevant to building agents that make decisions.

I've also contributed to research at **UConn**, the **University of Gothenburg**, and **ETH Zurich**, working on choice modeling and transportation systems.
